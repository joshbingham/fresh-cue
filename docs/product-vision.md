# FreshCue

## Vision

FreshCue helps people reduce food waste by making it easy to track what food they have, when it expires, and what should be used next.

Rather than simply storing inventory, FreshCue helps users make better decisions by highlighting priorities, reducing waste, and supporting meal planning.

## Target Users

- Busy households
- Students
- Families
- Anyone trying to reduce food waste and grocery spending

## Core Problem

People often forget what food they already own.

Items expire unnoticed.

Duplicate purchases happen.

Food is thrown away unnecessarily.

## Goal

Turn a fridge, freezer and cupboard into a searchable, prioritised inventory that helps users know what they have and what should be used next.
